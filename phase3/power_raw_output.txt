### What hierarchical pooling actually buys

Pooling does not make a single tail pair individually significant sooner — nothing does.
It makes the per-pair *estimate* useful (low MSE) long before it is significant. The
empirical-Bayes shrinkage factor is n/(n + 4/tau^2) with tau = the population SD of the
effect; the crossover where data outweighs the prior is n* = 4/tau^2.

synergy: tau=0.0707 -> n* = 801 co-occurrences = 7,327,180 matches at the median pair (measured Zipf s=1.154)
counter: tau=0.0632 -> n* = 1,001 co-occurrences = 6,105,983 matches at the median pair (measured Zipf s=1.154)
